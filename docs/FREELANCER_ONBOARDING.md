# 📖 Freelancer Onboarding: Breathe Collection Pipeline

Welcome to the Breathe Collection Growth Engine. This manual defines what we are looking for in creator partnerships and how to maintain our "Quiet Luxury" brand standards.

## 1. Visual Standards: "Quiet Luxury"
We target creators who prioritize quality over quantity.
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist, cinematic, high production value.
- **Vibe**: Grounded, authoritative, calm.
- **Avoid**: Loud graphics, neon text, messy/cluttered backgrounds, "gym bro" energy.

## 2. Niche & Profile Vetting
We are looking for **performance-based** creators in these niches:
- **Biohacking**: Oura ring users, sleep optimization, peak performance.
- **Science-backed Wellness**: Yoga instructors, breathwork specialists, meditation advocates.
- **High-Stress Careers**: Founders, executives, or creatives discussing burnout and focus.

## 3. Strict Safety (The Avoid List)
The Scout Agent is strictly prohibited from contacting:
- **Major Authorities**: Andrew Huberman, Wim Hof, and their official accounts.
- **Direct Competitors**: Creators heavily sponsored by Calm, Headspace, or direct breathwork competitors.
- **Medical Misinformation**: Creators making "cure" claims for serious diseases.

## 4. Operational Workflow (The Kanban Board)
- **Discovery**: AI-found leads. Perform a quick 5-second aesthetic check.
- **Approved**: High-confidence leads. Get a **Promo Code** using the button in the Mission Control panel.
- **Outreach Sent**: Initial message sent. Note the Promo Code in your outreach.
- **Rejected**: Use this for competitor accounts or low-quality visual profiles.

## 5. Promo Codes
Use the **"Get Promo Code"** button in the lead detail modal once a profile is marked as **Approved**. Copy this code into your outreach message to incentivize the creator to download the app.
